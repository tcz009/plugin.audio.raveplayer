# Station tiles — legal note (nominative use only)

**Status:** art study for owner approval. Not wired, not committed, not deployed.

These ten tiles are original drawings generated by `build_stations.py` from
primitives. They are meant to *evoke* the stations in Rave Player's Live Radio
rows; they are not official station artwork.

## What was not done

- No station logo, wordmark, screenshot or brand asset was downloaded, traced,
  vectorised, exported or pasted into the tiles.
- No wordmark is reproduced. Kool FM appears only as a small repeated code
  texture (`KOOL`) and a catalogue tag (`KOOL-01`), not as a logo. Rinse FM has
  no wordmark or type at all.
- The smiley, ripple rings, clock, fern, drum, siren, door, broken record and
  skull are generic rave/radio motifs, deliberately distorted; none is a trace
  of a station mark.
- No affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is claimed or implied.

## Real stations vs genre rows

- **Kool FM** and **Rinse FM** are real stations. Their names and any associated
  marks are trademarks of their owners. The tiles use the names descriptively
  (nominative use) to identify the station in a personal player.
- **Oldskool & Rave, Jungle, Drum & Bass, Hardcore, House, UK Garage,
  Breakbeat, Pirate Stations** are genre/category labels, not third-party
  brands. Their marks are original genre motifs.

## Using the art

- Keep the names descriptive and in the UI row context; do not use the tiles as
  station branding, merchandise, app icons, or promotional artwork for the
  stations.
- Do not imply the stations approved or supplied the art.
- If a station or rights-holder objects, replace the tile with a neutral genre
  mark. The art is deliberately an evocation, not a copy, so this is a
  rename/replace, not a redesign.
- Nominative use is a defence, not a licence. If the set is distributed beyond
  the personal player, review the station names and marks with the owner first.

## Scope

- The sheet is for owner approval only. No `resources/art/`, `routes.py`, tests,
  `preview.jpg`, git, or deploy changes are included.
- Not legal advice.
